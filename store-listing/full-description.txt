Place your fleet, read the board, and choose your next shot. Sinkpoint is a hidden-fleet strategy game for Android, with solo opponents and nearby multiplayer.

PLAY SOLO OR WITH SOMEONE NEARBY
Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard for a solo match. Easy gives you room to learn, while Normal and Hard use your shot history to hunt for the rest of your fleet. Solo play works offline.

For a two-player game, host or join a nearby match on separate Android devices. Compare the pairing code, agree on the rules, and keep your fleet hidden on your own screen. Nearby play uses Bluetooth and Wi-Fi through Google Play services; no internet connection or router is required.

CHOOSE YOUR RULES
Start with the familiar 10 by 10 board and five-piece fleet, or change the board size, fleet lengths, and whether pieces may touch. Custom boards range from 6 by 6 to 12 by 12.

Play Classic with one shot at a time, or choose Salvo to select one target for each surviving piece. In Classic, a hit gives you another shot by default. Turn that option off if you prefer to alternate after every shot.

PLACE, AIM, FIRE
Drag pieces onto the board and rotate them into position, or use tap-to-place controls. Random placement arranges the whole fleet for you, ready for any final adjustments.

Tap a target, check your choice, then press Fire. Hits, misses, sunk pieces, and fleet indicators keep the match easy to follow. Pinch to zoom and pan when you want a closer look.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Solo and nearby matches save separately on your device. Leave a game and return later, review recent results, and track your statistics. Export a backup of your settings, solo progress, and statistics through Android's file picker. Nearby matches stay on their original devices.

AT HOME ON ANDROID
Use light, dark, or device themes, with wallpaper colors on supported devices. Layouts adapt to phones, tablets, and landscape screens. Learn the rules in the built-in guide, adjust haptics, and use accessible board labels and actions.

No ads or accounts. Sinkpoint has no app analytics or game server. Nearby multiplayer requires Google Play services and Bluetooth/Wi-Fi permissions; Google's Nearby service may collect diagnostics as described in the privacy policy.
