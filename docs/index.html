01 / SOLO

Three ways to test your aim

Start with Easy, work through Normal, or take on Hard's fleet modeling and planned volleys. Your unfinished match is saved so you can return whenever you like.

02 / NEARBY

Two phones.
One match.

One player hosts, the other joins. Compare the pairing code, place your fleets, and play over a nearby Bluetooth or Wi-Fi connection. Multiplayer needs Google Play services and nearby permissions.

YOU⌁FRIEND

No server, account, or router required.

03 / YOUR RULES

Build your own battle

Use the classic 10 × 10 board or choose a board from 6 × 6 to 12 × 12. Customize your fleet, allow pieces to touch, or switch to salvo turns for a volley of shots.
