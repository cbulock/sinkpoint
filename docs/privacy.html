Privacy

Your games stay
on your device.

Sinkpoint has no accounts, ads, app analytics, or backend service. Solo games work offline. Nearby multiplayer connects two devices directly.

Updated October 13, 2026

Saved games and statistics

Settings, fleets, moves, unfinished matches, and statistics are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Nearby multiplayer

Nearby play uses Google's Nearby Connections through Google Play services. Sinkpoint requests Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and, on older Android versions, location permissions needed for discovery. Sinkpoint does not use these permissions to track your location.

The devices exchange pairing and match information, rules, shots, and results. Your hidden fleet remains on your device. No Sinkpoint server receives your game data.

Google's Nearby SDK can collect usage analytics and device information. That collection is separate from Sinkpoint's local saves and is controlled through Google's Usage & diagnostics setting. See Google's Nearby data collection documentation.

Export and import

Backups use Android's document picker. You choose where to save the file, which may be a local folder or a cloud provider. Backups contain preferences, solo progress, and statistics; they exclude nearby progress and device identity. Any sharing or cloud storage you choose is handled by that provider.

This website and downloads

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and APK downloads are hosted by GitHub Releases. The site adds no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub handles requests under its privacy statement.

Questions

For questions about the app, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public; avoid posting private information or backup files.
